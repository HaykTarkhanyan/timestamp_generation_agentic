## Lecturer mistakes

- At 0:07:33 she writes “has joins” for the directedness condition. The condition guarantees a common upper bound, which need not be a *least* upper bound.
- At 0:30:36 she says the neighborhood basis example needs closure under finite intersections. It does not: the basis property already gives a member contained in the intersection of any two members. Choosing an intersection-closed basis is valid but stronger than necessary.
- The directed-set definition at 0:04:42 does not state that the index set is nonempty. That condition is needed for the later claim that every net in a compact space has a cluster point.
