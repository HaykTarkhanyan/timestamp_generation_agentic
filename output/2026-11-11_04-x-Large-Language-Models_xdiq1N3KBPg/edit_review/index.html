How to apply this in YouTube Studio
  • Listen to after the cut first: it is 4 s before the cut joined to 4 s after, so you hear exactly how the edit will sound. What gets removed is the cut part itself, boosted so faint sounds are audible.
  • Untick anything you want to keep. Ticks are remembered in this browser. The minimum-silence menu at the top hides the shorter silence cuts.
  • In Studio: Editor → Trim & cut → New Cut for each line of the list in the last section. It uses Studio's own numbering (Cut 1..N by start time) and its H:MM:SS:FF frame times, the same as studio.html, listed last first. Studio keeps the original timeline while you edit, so the times stay valid.
  • Times are rounded inward to whole frames, so a cut never eats into speech.
  • Studio edits can't be undone after saving. Keep your original recording.
  • Chapters come after the edit: every cut shifts the times after it.

Silence = speech-band level below -74 dB (noise floor -94 dB + 20) for at least 3 s; clicks under 0.2 s count as silence. Each silence cut leaves 0.35 s of pause on both sides. Content cuts come from the transcript, with edges snapped to the speech edge.
