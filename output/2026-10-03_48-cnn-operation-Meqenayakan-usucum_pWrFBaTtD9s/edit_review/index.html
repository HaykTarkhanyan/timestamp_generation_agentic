How to apply this in YouTube Studio
  • Listen to after the cut first: it is 4 s before the cut joined to 4 s after, so you hear exactly how the edit will sound. What gets removed is the cut part itself, boosted so faint sounds are audible.
  • Untick anything you want to keep. Ticks are remembered in this browser. The minimum-silence menu at the top hides the shorter silence cuts.
  • In Studio: Editor → Trim & cut. Do the end trim first, then the cuts from the last one to the first, then the start trim. The list in the last section is already in that order. Working backwards means an earlier time never moves, whether or not Studio re-times the timeline after each cut.
  • Times are rounded inward to 0.1 s, so a rounded cut never eats into speech. If Studio's boxes only take whole seconds, round the start up and the end down.
  • Studio edits can't be undone after saving. Keep your original recording.
  • Chapters come after the edit: every cut shifts the times after it.

Silence = speech-band level below -74 dB (noise floor -94 dB + 20) for at least 2 s; clicks under 0.2 s count as silence. Each silence cut leaves 0.35 s of pause on both sides. Content cuts come from the transcript, with edges snapped to the speech edge.
