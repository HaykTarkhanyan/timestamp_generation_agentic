## Lecturer mistakes

- **0:38:36:** In the proof of the two-coloring criterion, she says or writes “because there are no cycles.” The hypothesis is **no odd cycles**; bipartite graphs can have even cycles.
- **1:43:23–1:45:04:** She claims that a Borel homomorphism from G_0 rules out a countable *Baire-measurable* coloring by composition. Baire measurability is not generally preserved when precomposing with a Borel map. The composition argument rules out a countable **Borel** coloring.
- **1:16:41–1:17:24:** Her claim that a Baire-measurable coloring must use continuum many colors goes beyond the proved “no countable coloring” result. The perfect-set argument she mentions applies to the image of a **Borel** map; it does not give that conclusion for an arbitrary Baire-measurable map. She partly retreats from the claim at 1:17:27–1:17:35.
