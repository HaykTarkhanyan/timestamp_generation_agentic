## Lecturer mistakes

- At 0:54:22, she calls “complete and totally bounded” the *Heine–Borel property*. That is nonstandard: for a metric space, the Heine–Borel property ordinarily says that every closed bounded subset is compact. For example, ℝ has that property but is not totally bounded. Her equivalence theorem is correct with “complete and totally bounded” as condition (3); the name warrants a correction note.
