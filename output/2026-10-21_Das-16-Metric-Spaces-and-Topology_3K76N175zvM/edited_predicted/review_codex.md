## Lecturer mistakes

- At 1:25:41, the claim that every \(b_k\) component is a tree overlooks periodic points. In particular, \(0\) is fixed; other periodic points give components with cycles.
- At 1:28:23–1:31:35, \(b_k\) is called isomorphic to the full left shift through base-\(k\) expansions. Expansions are ambiguous at \(k\)-adic rationals. A chosen canonical expansion identifies \(b_k\) with the shift on a proper invariant subset.
- At 1:41:18, “three colors suffice” needs a convention excluding loops: the defined graph contains the edge \(0\to0\), so it has no proper coloring as stated. The assertion that its components can each be colored with two colors also fails for components with odd cycles.
