How to read this

Each word of each title's topic is coloured by where it was found. Blue: he says it in that piece (auto-captions). Orange: he says it in another piece, or the course's published titles/chapters (1.1-20.3) use it, or only the glossary has it. Red: found nowhere, so possibly an invented word. Endings count as the same word (մատրիցը = մատրից); derived words do not (օրթոգոնալություն is not օրթոգոնալ). The captions garble words, so a red word may still be his: the evidence lists the closest words he does say. Hover a word for its source; open a title below for the lines. Words found nowhere: 0.
