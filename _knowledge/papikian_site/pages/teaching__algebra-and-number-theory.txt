Algebra and Number Theory
ASOF Honors Course (Fall 2024)
Course Information
This course is open to all students interested in Algebra and Number Theory. Some prior knowledge of Linear Algebra will be helpful but not necessary. The course will cover the basic notions of abstract algebra with a view toward applications in number theory. This course will be a preparation for Armenian students for the CIMPA summer school I am co-organizing in 2025.
Location: The auditorium of the Institute of Mathematics of the Academy of Sciences (24/5 Marshal Baghramyan Avenue, Yerevan).
Duration: September 13 - December 13, 2024.
Schedule: Tuesday and Friday every week, 17:00-19:00.
Each lecture will be 90 minutes long, followed by a 30-minute recitation (practice session).
I will join and deliver the lectures over zoom. The recitations will be taught by the course TA (Ruben Hambardzumyan) in person.
Prerequisites: Basic knowledge of English. I will lecture in Armenian but the slides will be in English. Some knowledge of Linear Algebra will be helpful but not necessary.
Grades: The course will have weekly homework assignments, one midterm exam and a final exam.
